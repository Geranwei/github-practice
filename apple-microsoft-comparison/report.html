Apple vs. Microsoft

FY2022–FY2026 · Consolidated results · Filings through November 21, 2026

Apple posted higher revenue in all five years. Microsoft grew revenue faster and earned a higher net margin in every year. In FY2026, Apple reported $478.8 billion in revenue versus Microsoft’s $331.8 billion. Microsoft grew 17.8% versus Apple’s 15.1%, and its net margin reached 40.3% versus Apple’s 27.6%.

Method and SEC sources

Years align by fiscal-year label. Microsoft closes on June 30 and Apple closes on the last Saturday of September. Apple FY2023 had 53 weeks. Figures use reported periods.

Growth is the percentage change from prior-year revenue. Net margin is consolidated GAAP net income divided by revenue. FY2022 growth uses FY2021 revenue, and FY2024 growth uses FY2023 revenue from the FY2023 filings below. Ratios use filing precision and display one decimal place. All inputs are available and all denominators are nonzero.

Apple’s Total net sales and Net income come from the Consolidated Statements of Operations. FY2023 Form 10-K · FY2026 Form 10-K
Microsoft’s Total revenue and Net income come from the Income Statements. FY2023 Form 10-K · FY2026 Form 10-K

Microsoft’s segment recasts retained the consolidated revenue and net income figures used here. December 2024 recast · October 2026 recast
