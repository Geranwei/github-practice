# Apple vs Microsoft: five-year comparison

1. Compare Apple and Microsoft across each company’s latest five completed fiscal years. Show each fiscal-year end date and note that the companies’ reporting periods differ.
2. Collect revenue and GAAP net income from SEC filings, with direct source links. Collect the preceding year’s revenue where available to calculate growth for the first displayed year.
3. Present revenue in USD billions, annual revenue growth as (current-year revenue ÷ prior-year revenue − 1) × 100, and net margin as net income ÷ revenue × 100. Use unrounded values for calculations and n/a where required data is unavailable.
4. Prepare matching HTML and PDF reports with a comparison table, charts, concise findings, fiscal-year end dates, and source links.
5. Check figures against the filings, verify calculations and rounding, compare the two reports, test links, and inspect layout and legibility.

Keep this folder simple: task.md, source-data.csv, report.html, and report.pdf.
